# kkmaths-365.github.io
Welcome to the official digital portfolio and mathematics resource hub of Prof. Krushnkumar Soneji (K.K. Sir). As a dedicated Mathematics Faculty member at Gyanmanjari Career Institute in Surat, Gujarat, my mission is to make advanced mathematics accessible, engaging, and highly structured for secondary and higher secondary students.

This platform is designed specifically to support students preparing for Class 11 and Class 12 NCERT/GSEB board examinations, as well as competitive engineering entrance tests like JEE Mains and GUJCET.

Here, students and fellow educators will find a continuously updated collection of high-quality study materials, including:

Automated Daily Practice Problems (DPPs): Unique mathematics worksheets generated using Python and rendered perfectly with MathJax/LaTeX.

Comprehensive Revision Papers: Tailored mock exams and test papers engineered to solidify core concepts across algebra, calculus, and coordinate geometry.

Interactive Learning Tools: Dynamic quizzes and downloadable PDF lesson plans tailored for rigorous daily practice.

Drawing from my extensive classroom experience—from A Model School to Gyanmanjari Career Institute—I have structured these digital resources to bridge the gap between theoretical understanding and high-speed problem-solving. Whether you are a student aiming to maximize your board scores, preparing for the TAT-HS/TAT-S exams, or pushing for top percentiles in JEE, this platform provides the exact materials required for success.
