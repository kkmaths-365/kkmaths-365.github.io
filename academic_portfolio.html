About Me

Currently serving as Mathematics Faculty at Gyanmanjari Career Institute, I specialize in crafting comprehensive academic strategies for Class 11 and 12 students. My primary focus is bridging the gap between foundational NCERT concepts and the high-level analytical thinking required for competitive exams like JEE Mains and GUJCET.

My academic journey includes a Master of Science (M.Sc.) degree, providing me with a rigorous analytical framework. I have formally certified my pedagogical expertise by completing the preparation for the TAT-HS and TAT-S teacher aptitude exams. Prior to my current role, I honed my teaching methodologies at A Model School, where I developed a passion for student-centric education.
